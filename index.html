01 — About

I wasn't much of a student until one opportunity changed that.

Until 10th grade I wasn't especially engaged in school. That changed in 11th grade, when I was picked for my first Erasmus+ project. That's genuinely where things turned around—my teachers kept choosing me for whatever international project came up after that, mostly because I was reliable in English and stepped in early when something was about to go sideways. I'd rather fix problems quietly, step by step, than let noise build up around me, and all of that put me in a team-leader seat again and again.

From then until the end of high school I gravitated toward being the person who holds a room together: coordinating across languages and representing my school abroad. That opportunity gave me the vision to start taking action, which changed my life and brought me all the way to Poland.
